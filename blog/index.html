Smarter, More Distributed, More Diverse, and a New Role for RDMSes: The Next Revolution For Databases

The article explains how databases are evolving to handle much larger amounts of data and more complex workloads than before. It highlights how systems are becoming more distributed, meaning data is stored and processed across multiple locations/regions instead of a single server.


The article also points out that there are now many different types of databases, each designed for specific tasks like analytics, transactions, or real-time processing. It discusses how traditional relational databases are still important but are now working alongside newer database technologies rather than replacing them.


Overall, the article shows that modern databases are becoming more flexible, scalable, and better suited for today's data-driven applications.

How Database Operations Management Can Accelerate the Business with Nutanix

Managing databases using manual processes is often slow, complicated, and resource-heavy, which can delay innovation and increase operational risk. Tasks like patching, testing updates, and responding to developer requests can take weeks, while maintaining large and growing database systems across different environments adds even more complexity and strain on IT teams.


Nutanix Database Service (NDB) helps solve these problems by automating routine tasks and simplifying database management across hybrid and multi-cloud systems. It allows teams to quickly test and apply updates, reduce downtime, cut costs, and shift their focus from repetitive maintenance work to more strategic, innovative projects, ultimately delivering strong business value and return on investment.
